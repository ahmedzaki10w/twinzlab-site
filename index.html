How we build The same four steps for our own apps and the ones we take on with others.

  1. Discover

    We start from a real problem, ours or one you bring to us.

  2. Design

    We shape the product until it is clear and simple to use.

  3. Develop

    We build, try it, and refine what we learn.

  4. Launch

    We ship, then keep the door open for what should come next.
